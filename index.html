Meet Egghub

Quality eggs should be affordable for every home.

At Egghub Foods, we bring farm-fresh eggs directly to your table and shops at the lowest possible prices.

We source straight from the farm and skip the long chain of middlemen, so families, shop owners and food businesses get fresher eggs for less. From our base in Mowe, Ogun State, we serve customers across Ogun and Lagos.

Our mission is simple: to make quality eggs affordable and accessible to every household.
